# 🧠 1. What is a *clitic*?

A **clitic** is a **small word that cannot stand alone** and must attach to a verb.

In Portuguese, these are mainly:
- **me, te, se, nos, vos, o, a, lhe, etc.**

👉 Example:
- **me** divirto  
- **se** lembra  
- **nos** levantamos  

These little pieces (**me / se / nos**) are **clitic pronouns**.

---

# 🔗 2. How clitics connect to reflexive verbs

Reflexive verbs **require a reflexive clitic**.

👉 So:
- **se divertir** = verb (**divertir**) + clitic (**se**)

Without the clitic, the meaning is wrong or incomplete.

---

### Compare:

- Eu **divirto** → ❌ (sounds wrong / incomplete)
- Eu **me divirto** → ✅ correct

👉 The clitic is **not optional** — it’s structurally required.

---

# 🧩 3. Why clitics exist (key idea)

Clitics encode:
- **who receives the action**
- often **reflexive meaning**

👉 Think of them as:
> “compressed pronouns attached to the verb”

---

# ⚙️ 4. Position of clitics (this is where it gets interesting)

In Brazilian Portuguese, clitics usually come **before the verb**.

### ✅ Most common (proclisis)
- Eu **me lembro**
- Você **se diverte**
- Nós **nos levantamos**

---

### ⚠️ Sometimes after the verb (more formal / written)
- lembrar-**me**
- divertir-**se**

👉 Example:
- É importante **lembrar-se** disso

---

### ⚠️ With infinitives (very common pattern)
- para **se divertir**
- antes de **se levantar**

---

# 🔁 5. Reflexive vs non-reflexive via clitics

Clitics can **change meaning**:

### lembrar vs lembrar-se

- lembrar → to remind  
- lembrar-**se** → to remember  

👉 Example:
- Eu **me lembro** → I remember  
- Eu lembro você → I remind you (rare in speech)

---

### esquecer vs esquecer-se

- Eu **esqueci o livro** (no clitic, direct object)
- Eu **me esqueci do livro** (reflexive form, more natural)

👉 In Brazil:
➡️ Both exist, but **reflexive is VERY common**

---

# 🎯 6. Types of clitics (important distinction)

## 1. Reflexive clitics
Used when subject = object

- me, se, nos  
👉 Eu **me levanto**

---

## 2. Object clitics
Used for direct/indirect objects

- o, a, lhe  

👉 Eu **o vejo** (I see him)

---

⚠️ Same mechanism, different function

# ⚠️ Subtle but important: personal infinitive

With **nós**, Portuguese prefers:

👉 **nos levantarmos** (not *nos levantar*)

### ✔ Correct:
👉 **Antes de nos levantarmos cedo...**

---

# 🧠 Why?

Portuguese has something English doesn’t:

👉 **personal infinitive**

| Subject | Form |
|--------|------|
| nós | levantar**mos** |

---

# 🔥 Clean rule

If you use:

👉 **nós + verb in a sentence**

Then:
- ❌ don’t use infinitive  
- ✅ use conjugated form
